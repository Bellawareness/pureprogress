# 🌍 Travels Feature - Quick Start Guide

## Access Your Travel Journal
1. Open your habit tracker app
2. Click the **🌍 Travels** button in the navigation bar (next to Today and History)

## Add a City You Visited
1. Fill in the **City Name** (required, e.g., "Paris")
2. Add the **Country** (optional, e.g., "France")
3. Select **When did you visit?** (month/year of your visit)
4. Write **Notes** about the place (memories, favorite food, etc.)
5. Click **"Add City"**

## Find a City You Remember
- Use the search box at the top of the "Search & Filter" section
- Type the city name or country
- Results show instantly as you type

## Sort Your Travel List
Choose from the dropdown menu:
- **Most Recent** - Show newest visits first
- **Oldest First** - Show your earliest travels first  
- **Alphabetical (A-Z)** - Cities in A-Z order
- **By Country** - Group cities by country

## See Your Travel Stats
At the top of the Travels tab, you'll see:
- 🌐 **Total Cities Visited** - Count of unique cities
- 🗺️ **Total Countries** - How many countries you've been to

## Each City Card Shows
- City name and country (big text at top)
- 📅 Date visited (formatted nicely)
- Your personal notes about the place
- When you added this entry
- Delete button if you want to remove it

## Tips

### For Cities from Long Ago
- Just add the month and year you remember (e.g., "June 2015")
- Don't worry if you don't remember the exact date - an approximate date is fine!
- Add detailed notes about what you remember

### For Organized Tracking
- Add countries consistently (helps with sorting)
- Write memorable details in notes (favorite restaurant, landmark, feeling)
- Regularly update as you travel

### Best Practices
✅ Add cities soon after visiting while memories are fresh
✅ Include country name for better organization
✅ Write specific memories in notes ("Tried amazing pasta", "Climbed the Eiffel Tower")
✅ Use search to find cities and reminisce

---

Your travel journal is now ready! Start adding the cities you've visited. 🌍✈️
