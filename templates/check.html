My Journey

The Beginning

Growing up in Linden, Guyana, my fascination with computers began early. I remember the excitement of attending computer classes in my hometown, and learning about hardware and software. Little did I know, this exposure would shape my future in profound ways. When my family moved to the United States, my passion for technology only intensified. Despite the challenges of adjusting to a new life, I immersed myself in the world of computers, even participating in a cutting-edge college Cyber Tech program while still in high school.

Learning & Growing

Throughout my educational journey, I maintained an unrelenting curiosity. In college, I stumbled upon web development, initially as an academic requirement. But it soon turned into a passion project that evolved into a thriving side business. Each website I built took me deeper into the realm of digital creation, helping me understand how technology can turn ideas into profitable digital experiences. As my skills grew, so did my ambition.

Today & Beyond

Today, I am fully immersed in the world of creativity and innovation. Combining my expertise in web development and A.I. with a strong design sense, I bring imagination to life, whether through sleek corporate websites or useful e-commerce platforms. The journey from an avid learner to a digital creator has been life changing. As I continue to hone my craft and expand my horizons, I am filled with excitement for the future, eager to push the boundaries of what is possible and leave my mark on the digital landscape.
