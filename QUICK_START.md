# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - HabitTracker

## First Time Setup

### 1. **Create Your Account**
- Click "Sign Up" (top right)
- Enter email and password
- Verify email (check inbox)
- Login

### 2. **Start Logging Data**

#### Daily Check-in
1. Click **Reflections & Insights** tab
2. Set your **mood** (😊, 😐, 😢)
3. Set your **energy level** (1-10)
4. Add any **notes**
- ✅ Auto-saves to cloud

#### Track Activities
1. Click **Habits & Activities** tab
2. Click on any activity (Workout, Reading, Meditation, etc.)
3. Click the **checkmark button** to mark complete
4. Streaks automatically calculate

#### Log Spending
1. Click **Finance & Spending** tab
2. Enter amount spent
3. Select category
- ✅ Auto-synced across devices

#### Create Custom Card
1. Click **My Cards** tab
2. Click **+ Create New Card**
3. Choose tracking type:
   - 🔥 **Streak**: Daily checkbox (build consecutive days)
   - 📊 **Count**: Total counter (track total times)
   - 📝 **Notes**: Info only (no tracking)
4. Pick color theme
5. Save!

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Will my data sync between devices?
**A:** Yes! Login on any device and all data syncs automatically. Newer data is kept, older data is replaced.

### Q: What if I lose internet?
**A:** App still works! Your data saves locally. When you get internet back, everything syncs to cloud.

### Q: Can I see my data history?
**A:** Yes! Each custom card shows when it was edited and what changed. Check browser console (F12) for sync logs.

### Q: How do I backup my data?
**A:** All data is backed up to Firebase automatically. Export feature coming soon.

### Q: Can I delete my account?
**A:** Yes, go to Settings (gear icon). All your data will be deleted.

### Q: Is this free?
**A:** Currently yes! May have premium features later ($5-10/mo) for advanced analytics.

## 📞 Troubleshooting

### App is slow?
- Clear browser cache
- Refresh page
- Check internet connection

### Data not showing up?
- Refresh page (Cmd+R or Ctrl+R)
- Logout and login again
- Check browser console (F12) for errors

### Sync not working?
- Check Firebase config in console
- Verify you're logged in
- Check internet connection
- Try logging out and back in

### Delete button not showing?
- Desktop: Press **Ctrl+Shift+D**
- Mobile: Long-press (1+ second) anywhere on page
