# Enable Email/Password Login in Firebase

## Quick Setup (2 minutes):

### Step 1: Enable Email/Password Authentication

1. Go to [Firebase Console](https://console.firebase.google.com/)
2. Click your **"learn-thyself"** project
3. In left menu, click **"Authentication"**
4. Click the **"Sign-in method"** tab
5. Find **"Email/Password"** in the list
6. Click on it
7. Toggle **"Enable"** to ON
8. Click **"Save"**

That's it! Email/password login is now enabled.

---

## How to Use Your New Login System:

### First Time Setup:

1. **Open your habit tracker** on any device
2. You'll see a **login screen**
3. Click **"Sign Up"** tab
4. Enter your email and password (min 6 characters)
5. Click **"Create Account"**
6. ✅ Your account is created!

### On Other Devices:

1. **Open your habit tracker** on iPad, phone, etc.
2. Click **"Login"** tab (already selected)
3. Enter the **same email/password**
4. Click **"Login"**
5. ✅ All your data appears automatically!

### Sharing with Family/Friends:

**Option 1: Shared Account** (Everyone sees the same data)
- Give them your email/password
- They login and see your shared habit tracker
- Perfect for couples or families tracking together

**Option 2: Separate Accounts** (Private data)
- They click "Sign Up" and create their own account
- Each person has their own private habit tracker
- Data is completely separate

## Troubleshooting:

**"Email already in use"**
- That email already has an account
- Use the Login tab instead

**"Invalid email or password"**
- Double-check your email/password
- Email is case-sensitive

**"Password must be at least 6 characters"**
- Use a longer password

**Lost your password?**
